What MLS Professionals Do

Medical Laboratory Scientists are detectives and problem solvers who use their knowledge and technical skills to conduct a wide variety of medical laboratory tests and then correlate the results with disease processes. They are experts in monitoring quality-control programs and also possess the technical skills to operate and maintain laboratory instruments.

Are You a Lab Detective?

This career could be a great match for you if you:

  • 🧪 Enjoy science and discovering how things work
  • 🧩 Like solving puzzles and figuring out clues
  • 🧠 Have fun thinking through problems step by step
  • ❤️ Want to help people, even if you’re working behind the scenes

Medical lab professionals are like “science detectives” who help doctors find answers. Even though they don’t always work directly with patients, their work is very important for saving lives.

MD Anderson MLS Program

🏫

School of Health Professions – Medical Laboratory Science

The University of Texas MD Anderson Cancer Center School of Health Professions offers a Bachelor of Science in Medical Laboratory Science — a two-year, upper-division program that combines rigorous classroom instruction with hands-on clinical rotations at leading laboratories across the Texas Medical Center.

Students gain expertise across all major laboratory disciplines including blood banking, bone marrow, clinical chemistry, flow cytometry, hematology & hemostasis, microbiology, and stem cell transplantation. The curriculum emphasizes critical thinking, quality control, and the correlation of laboratory results with disease processes.

Graduates are eligible to sit for the Board of Certification (BOC) exam through the American Society for Clinical Pathology (ASCP) and are prepared for rewarding careers in hospitals, reference labs, research institutions, and more.
