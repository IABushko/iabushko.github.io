Hounsfield Unit Scale

The CT scanner does not just see shapes. It also measures how strongly each part blocks X-rays. That measurement becomes a Hounsfield Unit. Then the computer turns HU numbers into grayscale: low numbers look dark, high numbers look bright. That helps us tell if the hidden object is more like air, water, soft tissue, bone, or metal.
